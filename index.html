● CHAPTER 01 · THE FOUNDATION

Good clothes, honest prices, zero brand pretension.

We started in Bangalore in 2013 with a straightforward conviction: Indian youth deserve high-energy, well-cut menswear that looks sharp without paying an inflated tax for a foreign label or a logo. Anant Tanted bootstrapped INDIAN GARAGE out of a genuine appreciation for everyday Indian street culture—designing clothes meant for real weather, diverse body types, and active days. Today, backed by Aditya Birla Group’s TMRW and under the roof of Styleverse Lifestyle, we remain true to that day-one promise: approachable style, honest fabrics, and undeniable value.

🏛️ Supported by Aditya Birla Group (TMRW) 🧵 Honest Fits & Everyday Fabrics 📍 Born in Bangalore, 2013
● CHAPTER 02 · RETAIL FOOTPRINT

24 doors open across 13 Indian cities.

While we began as a digital pioneer, clothes are fundamentally tactile. You want to feel the weight of the cotton, check the collar, and see how the jacket falls before stepping out. From Bangalore’s bustling Brigade Road and Mumbai to Lucknow, Kochi, Hyderabad, and Jaipur, our 24 physical stores are friendly, welcoming neighborhood spaces. More than 21,000 customers have rated their local store on Google, holding an average 4.9★ rating because our store teams treat every visitor with warmth and respect.

📍 24 Verified Exclusive Stores ⭐ 4.9 ★ Average Across 21,000+ Reviews 🌆 13 Metro & Regional Hubs
● CHAPTER 03 · CRAFT & VELOCITY

Cut in small batches. Never sitting in warehouses.

Legacy fashion brands place massive orders six months in advance. When customer tastes shift, warehouses fill up with dead stock and clothes get marked down or thrown away. We took the opposite approach. We design in agile micro-batches with partner workshops across India, turning fresh style ideas into racked garments in just 14 days. Every seam, wash, and dye bath passes strict inspection before it leaves the workshop. The result is an 88% sell-through within weeks—meaning our clothes spend their lives being worn, not sitting on clearance racks.

⏱️ 14-Day Design-to-Rack Turnaround 🔍 Hands-On Seam & Wash Inspection ♻️ 88% Sold Within Weeks · Minimal Waste
● CHAPTER 04 · OUR HORIZON

From day-one savings to ₹204 Cr scale.

Bootstrapped in 2013, INDIAN GARAGE has doubled revenue to ₹204 Crore in FY25 while remaining disciplined, profitable, and grounded in community trust. We never believed a brand had to pretend to be European or artificially exclusive to win. Joined by cricket icon Suryakumar Yadav (SKY) as our brand ambassador and expanding toward 100 standalone stores across India, we are building a lasting homegrown fashion house that celebrates Indian energy, street grit, and straightforward craft.
