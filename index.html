What We Develop: 6 Core Menswear Pillars

Moving 550,000+ garments every month demands a synchronized supply network of world-class textile mills, knitting facilities, wash laundries, and agile CMT units. Explore our active development categories, technical specifications, and vendor engagement standards below:

550k+ Units Monthly
AQL 1.5 Quality Standard

CAT 01 // WOVEN ~160,000 Units/Mo

Casual & Resort Shirts

High-turnover casual shirts, printed resort Cuban collars, linen-blends, brushed twill overshirts, and textured corduroys for year-round everyday wear.

  • Fabrics 100% Combed Cotton, Linen Blends, Rayon Poplin
  • Yarn Counts 40s, 50s, 60s Compact / 2/80s Twill
  • Quality Baseline AQL 1.5 • Pre-shrunk (≤3%) • Colorfast Grade 4+
CAT 02 // KNITS ~100,000 Units/Mo

Polos & Knitted Tops

Signature pique polos, mercerised cotton polos, waffle textured knits, and modern minimal zipper-neck collars for smart-casual wardrobes.

  • Fabrics 220–260 GSM Honeycomb Pique, Mercerised Cotton
  • Finishes Bio-Polish, Anti-Pilling, Jacquard Collar Rib
  • Quality Baseline Spun Dye Resistance • Reinforced Placket Fusing
CAT 03 // STREET ~120,000 Units/Mo

Tees & Graphic Streetwear

Heavyweight oversized tees, drop-shoulder silhouettes, vintage mineral acid-washes, high-density puff graphics, and clean essentials.

  • Fabrics 200–280 GSM Single Jersey, Combed Organic Cotton
  • Print Tech HD Puff, Discharge, Silicone Gel, Acid Vintage Dip
  • Quality Baseline Lycra Rib Collar Recovery • Zero Twisting
CAT 04 // BOTTOMS ~90,000 Units/Mo

Denim & Utility Bottoms

Carpenter pants with authentic hammer loops, relaxed wide-leg jeans, distressed enzyme washes, stretch cotton chinos, and 6-pocket cargo joggers.

  • Fabrics 11.0 – 13.5 oz Ring-Spun Denim, Cotton-Elastane Twill
  • Wash Techniques Ozone Eco-Wash, Enzyme Stone Wash, Whisker Scrapes
  • Quality Baseline Reinforced Bar-Tacking • Heavy Metal Hardware
CAT 05 // KNITWEAR ~45,000 Units/Mo

Winterwear & Flat Knits

Geometric intarsia crew necks, cable-knit sweaters, 320+ GSM brushed fleece hoodies, varsity pullovers, and lightweight zip cardigans.

  • Gauge / Yarns 7GG & 12GG Flat Bed, Cotton-Acrylic, Brushed Poly-Fleece
  • Specs Anti-Shrink Steam Treatment, Thermal Brushed Back
  • Quality Baseline Hem Retraction Integrity • Colorfastness Grade 4
CAT 06 // OUTERWEAR ~35,000 Units/Mo

Outerwear & Technical Jackets

Down-touch polyfill puffer jackets, water-resistant windbreakers, quilted bomber jackets, and heavy denim trucker jackets built for modern commutes.

  • Materials Ripstop Micro-Poly, Thermal Bonded Wadding, DWR Coated
  • Details Heat-Sealed Baffles, Stormproof Cuffs, Waterproof Zippers
  • Quality Baseline Thermal Fill Uniformity • Zero Fiber Migration
