THE REVOLUTION

The "Zero Brand Tax" Philosophy

In 2012, young Indian men faced a binary trap: pay a 400% inflated price for an imported label just to wear an embroidered logo, or settle for uninspired domestic garments with poor fits and low-grade poly-mix fabrics. Anant Tanted established Indian Garage on a non-negotiable premise: Zero Brand Tax. Every single rupee goes into 13.5oz ring-spun denim warp, 320 GSM combed cotton, double-needle lock stitching, and calibrated South Asian rise ergonomics.

✓ Honest Value • 100% Product Integrity • Zero Logo Markup
OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE

100+ Decentralized Agile Factory Pods

While legacy brands built monolithic, debt-heavy garment factories with 6-month rigid scheduling, Indian Garage architected a modern, asset-light manufacturing web across Indore, Surat, and Tirupur. Over 100 specialized decentralized pods execute synchronized micro-batches. This eliminates capital-intensive dead stock, slashes idle factory capacity, and powers our legendary 14-day concept-to-rack velocity machine.
