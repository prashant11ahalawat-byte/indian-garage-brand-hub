01 / INVESTMENT THESIS

Who We Are: The Valuation & Growth Story

Founded in 2012 by Anant Tanted in Indore, The Indian Garage Co. dismantled the myth that trend-first, runway-grade fashion had to carry an international luxury price tag.

FOUNDER ROOTS & EARLY BOOTSTRAP

Starting with a modest ₹10 Lakh government loan and a 4-member team in Indore, Anant Tanted recognized the massive void in Indian menswear: young men wanted the sharp, aggressive cuts of Milan and Tokyo runways, but domestic brands offered stale fits, while global labels charged inflated markups. TIGC built dedicated localized supply pods to bring high-energy trends to market in real-time.

ADITYA BIRLA GROUP / TMRW PARTNERSHIP

In October 2023, TMRW (the digital venture of Aditya Birla Fashion & Retail Ltd.) invested ₹155 Crore ($18.6 Million) for a strategic stake in The Indian Garage Co. This landmark institutional partnership unlocked unprecedented enterprise scale: shared logistics infrastructure, prime retail real estate leverage across premier Indian shopping malls, and advanced AI-led merchandising algorithms.

03 / HERO CATEGORY

The Denim Engine: 2,800,000+ Pairs Sold

Denim is the foundational soul of The Indian Garage Co. We engineered a proprietary fit specifically tailored to South Asian male morphology.

PROPRIETARY INDIAN FIT MORPHOLOGY
  • Ergonomic Rise & Anti-Gaping Waist: International cuts gap at the back or pinch the groin. TIGC denims feature a calibrated rise that stays locked to the hips whether sitting, riding a motorcycle, or walking.
  • Contoured Thigh & Calibrated Inseam: Accommodates athletic Indian thighs without flaring at the ankle, providing a sharp tailored taper.
  • 2% High-Recovery Elastane: 13.5oz ring-spun denim with 2% Lycra memory stretch that withstands 200+ washes without sagging at the knees.
SELL-THROUGH VELOCITY & SUSTAINABILITY
  • 88% Full-Price Sell-Through: Our denim inventory turns over 6.2x annually, with 88% of drop inventory clearing at full retail price within 45 days.
  • Ozone & Laser Wash Technology: Reducing industrial water consumption by 65% across our washing facilities, utilizing ozone bleaching instead of harsh chemical chlorine.
  • Reinforced Bartack Stitching: 14 structural stress points reinforced with military-grade 100% core-spun polyester thread.
