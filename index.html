● LAYER 01 // 04 · FOUNDATION & IDENTITY

Menswear Built on Authenticity, Not Brand Tax.

Founded in Bangalore in 2013 by Anant Tanted, The Indian Garage Co. began with a bold conviction: Indian youth deserve high-energy, contemporary fast fashion without the inflated price tags of legacy brands. Operating under Styleverse Lifestyle Private Limited and fortified by a landmark ₹155 Crore growth partnership with Aditya Birla Group’s TMRW, our foundation unites agile entrepreneurial spirit with enterprise corporate governance.

🏛️ Aditya Birla (TMRW) Union ⚡ Styleverse Lifestyle Pvt. Ltd. 🏷️ Zero Brand Tax Philosophy
● LAYER 02 // 04 · NATIONWIDE RETAIL FOOTPRINT

24 Landmark Stores Across 13 Cities.

From Bangalore’s iconic Brigade Road and Lucknow’s premier Phoenix Palassio to Lulu Malls in Kochi and Trivandrum, Indian Garage has evolved from a digital-first pioneer into a vibrant physical retail destination. Every store is designed as a cultural home for modern streetwear, validated by over 21,000 verified Google reviews averaging an extraordinary 4.9★ rating.

📍 24 Verified Exclusive Outlets ⭐ 4.9 ★ Average Google Rating 🌆 13 Prime Metro & Tier Hubs
● LAYER 03 // 04 · CRAFT, VELOCITY & SCALE

14-Day Velocity & AQL 1.5 Precision.

Fashion speed only endures when quality and fit are uncompromising. Coordinated across over 100 decentralized agile factory pods, we move from initial trend intelligence to racked garments in just 14 days. Operating at a strict AQL 1.5 inspection threshold, our micro-batch model yields an exceptional 88% sell-through within 45 days—eliminating obsolete dead stock and ensuring constantly fresh collections.

⚡ 14-Day Concept-to-Rack Speed 🛡️ AQL 1.5 Quality Standard 📦 550,000+ Garments Sold Monthly
● LAYER 04 // 04 · OUR JOURNEY & HORIZON

From 2013 Roots to ₹204 Cr Scale & Beyond.

Having doubled our net revenue to ₹204 Cr in FY25 while remaining profitable, The Indian Garage Co. is on track toward 100 exclusive stores nationwide. Endorsed by cricket icon Suryakumar Yadav (SKY), we are building a generational Indian fashion house that celebrates street culture, craftsmanship, and unpretentious cool.

📈 ₹204 Cr FY25 Net Revenue 🎯 100 EBO Nationwide Roadmap 🏏 Suryakumar Yadav (SKY) Ambassador
