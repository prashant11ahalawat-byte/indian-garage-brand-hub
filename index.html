THE REVOLUTION

The "Zero Brand Tax" Philosophy

In 2012, young Indian men faced a binary trap: pay a 400% inflated price for an imported label just to wear an embroidered logo, or settle for uninspired domestic garments with poor fits and low-grade poly-mix fabrics. Anant Tanted established The Indian Garage Co. on a non-negotiable premise: Zero Brand Tax. Every single rupee goes into 13.5oz ring-spun denim warp, 320 GSM combed cotton, double-needle lock stitching, and calibrated South Asian rise ergonomics.

100+ Decentralized Agile Factory Pods
